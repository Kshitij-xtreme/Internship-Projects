Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose between term and whole-life insurance? +

It depends on your goals — term plans offer high cover at low cost for pure protection, while whole-life plans combine protection with savings. Our advisors help you decide based on your budget and goals.

Is the consultation really free? +

Yes, your first consultation with our certified advisors is completely free with no obligation to purchase.

How long does a claim usually take? +

Most health claims are settled within 48-72 hours when documentation is complete; our team actively follows up with insurers on your behalf.

Can I switch my existing mutual fund SIPs through BNI? +

Yes, we can help you review, consolidate, and switch existing SIPs into a better-aligned portfolio at no extra cost.
