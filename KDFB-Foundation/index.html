Who We Are

Rooted in Devotion,
Driven by Service

Founded under the divine inspiration of Lord Krishna's teachings, Krrishna Dhwarka Foundation Bharat believes that seva — selfless service — is the highest form of worship.

We operate with full transparency: every rupee donated is tracked, receipts are issued instantly, and impact reports are published quarterly.

  • Registered NGO under Societies Act, India
  • 80G & 12A Tax Exemption Certified
  • Zero administrative fees above 8%
  • Quarterly public impact reports
