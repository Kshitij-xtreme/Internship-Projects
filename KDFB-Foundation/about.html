Our Foundation

Inspired by Devotion,
Built on Service

Krrishna Dhwarka Foundation Bharat was established in 2017 with a singular vision: to be the bridge between privilege and potential. Guided by the timeless teachings of the Bhagavad Gita — that karma (righteous action) without expectation is the highest calling — our founders committed to a life of service.

From a single village programme in Gujarat, we have grown into a multi-state organisation serving over 12,400 lives. Our approach is grassroots-first: we live and work in the communities we serve.

  • Registered under the Societies Registration Act
  • FCRA-compliant for international donations
  • 80G & 12A income tax exemption certified
  • Annual third-party audit published publicly
  • Zero tolerance for administrative wastage

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

🪔

Seva (Selfless Service)

Every action we take is done without expectation of reward. Service is our worship.

📚

Jnana (Knowledge)

Education is liberation. We believe no child should be denied the light of learning.

🤝

Satya (Truth & Transparency)

Every rupee is tracked. Every report is public. We are accountable to those we serve.

🌱

Ahimsa (Non-harm)

Our programmes respect the dignity, culture, and autonomy of every community.
