        Dedicated Team Plan

          The Dedicated Team Plan is ideal for long-term projects or ongoing development needs. With this model, you hire a team of AI developers who work exclusively on your project, providing full attention and expertise. This plan ensures seamless integration with your internal team, fostering collaboration, continuity, and deeper understanding of your business goals. It's perfect for complex, large-scale AI projects that require consistent effort and dedication.

        Hourly Plan

          The Hourly Plan offers flexibility for businesses that need AI development expertise on a more ad-hoc or short-term basis. You can hire developers for specific tasks, feature enhancements, or short-term support, paying only for the hours worked. This model is cost-effective for businesses that don’t require a full-time team but still need high-quality AI solutions on demand, with the freedom to scale resources as needed.

        Contract Basis

          The Contract Basis model is a flexible option where you hire AI developers for a set period or project. The developer works on your AI project for the duration of the contract, with clearly defined objectives and timelines. This model is suitable for businesses with specific project requirements or short-term goals, offering the flexibility to bring in specialized talent for particular tasks while controlling costs.
