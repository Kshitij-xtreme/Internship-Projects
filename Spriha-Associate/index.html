WHO WE ARE

From micro components
to macro automation systems.

Spriha Associate, based in Vadodara, Gujarat, is one of the region's most established industrial electronics equipment repairing centers and a trusted solution provider for industrial automation.

We repair any type of industrial electronic board. Using state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment, our technicians troubleshoot faults down to the component level — not just the board level — which means more failures get fixed instead of replaced. We maintain a wide stock list of replacement parts to ensure quick turnaround on every job.

We specialise in all types of PCB repair work, delivered quickly, with warranty, and at low cost. Our engineers are trained on the latest test and repair technology, so your equipment goes back into service reliable and verified.
